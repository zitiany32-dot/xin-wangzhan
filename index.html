THE FACTORY

One site.
Everything under its roof.

Wire rod comes in one end of our Yongnian plant and containers leave the other. Heading, thread rolling, heat treatment, plating, inspection — all done here, by our own people. That is why we can promise a lead time and sign the certificate ourselves, instead of passing your order down a chain of workshops you will never see.

HOW IT GETS MADE

Six steps,
none outsourced.

01

Raw material

Wire rod yard

SAE 1035–1045 cold-heading wire. Every coil arrives with a mill certificate, and the heat number follows it through the whole plant.

02

Cold heading

12 lines

Multi-station presses stamp the head shape at room temperature. M3 to M36. Fast, and no metal wasted.

03

Thread rolling

±0.01 mm pitch

Threads are rolled, not cut — squeezed between hardened dies. Stronger thread, cleaner finish.

04

Heat treatment

Quench & temper

Continuous mesh-belt furnace. This is what turns a 4.8 into a 10.9 — hardness you can measure.

05

Surface coating

Our own lines

Zinc, hot-dip galvanising, Dacromet, black oxide. Salt spray tested up to 1000 hours in our lab.

06

Inspection

Before the seal

Tensile, hardness, torque — every batch. SGS or BV welcome at the plant while the container is still open.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked all the time.
Answered honestly.

What is the minimum order?

One carton, if it is a stock item. Made-to-order starts around 500 kg for common sizes — tell us the size and we will give you the exact number with the quote.

How long does it take?

Stock: 7–10 days. Production: 15–35 days depending on quantity and coating. Hot-dip galvanising adds about 5 days. The quote states a firm date, not a range.

Samples?

Free for standard items — you cover the courier. For a drawing part we make a sample first; if you order, the sample cost comes off the invoice.

Payment terms?

30% deposit, balance against the copy bill of lading for first orders. L/C at sight is fine. Regulars move to open account.

Do I get test certificates?

Every shipment: mill certificate traceable to the heat number. Third-party inspection (SGS, BV, TÜV, or your own man) arranged at our plant before sealing.

Can you make it from my drawing?

Yes. PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP — or just send a sample and we will measure it.
