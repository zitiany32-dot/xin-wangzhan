INSIDE THE PLANT

Six steps,
none outsourced.

01

Raw material

Wire rod yard

SAE 1035–1045 cold-heading wire. Every coil arrives with a mill certificate; the heat number follows it through the plant.

02

Cold heading

12 lines

Multi-station presses stamp the head at room temperature. M3 to M36 — fast, no metal wasted.

03

Thread rolling

±0.01 mm pitch

Threads rolled, not cut — squeezed between hardened dies. Stronger thread, cleaner finish.

04

Heat treatment

Quench & temper

Continuous mesh-belt furnace. This is what turns a 4.8 into a 10.9 — hardness you can measure.

05

Surface coating

Our own lines

Zinc, hot-dip galvanising, Dacromet, black oxide. Salt spray tested up to 1000 hours in our lab.

06

Inspection

Before the seal

Tensile, hardness, torque — every batch. SGS or BV welcome at the plant while the container is still open.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Asked all the time.
Answered honestly.

What is the minimum order?

One carton for stock items. Made-to-order starts around 500 kg for common sizes — send the size and the quote states the exact MOQ.

How long does production take?

Stock: 7–10 days. Production: 15–35 days depending on quantity and coating. Hot-dip galvanising adds about 5 days. The quote states a firm date, not a range.

Can I get samples first?

Free for standard items — you cover the courier. For a drawing part we make a sample first; order, and the sample cost comes off the invoice.

What payment terms do you accept?

30% deposit, balance against the copy bill of lading for first orders. L/C at sight is fine. Regulars move to open account.

Do I get test certificates?

Every shipment: mill certificate traceable to the heat number. Third-party inspection (SGS, BV, TÜV, or your own man) arranged at our plant before sealing.

Can you make it from my drawing?

Yes. PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP — or send a sample and we will measure it.
