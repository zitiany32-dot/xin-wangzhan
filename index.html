THE WORKS

Everything above this line
came off these floors.

Steel comes in one end of our Yongnian site and leaves the other in a container. Cold heading, hot forging, thread rolling, heat treatment, plating and inspection — every process under one roof, which is why the lead time is ours to promise and the certificate is ours to sign.

MANUFACTURING

Six stations.
Zero subcontracting.

01

Raw Material

Wire Rod

SAE 1035–1045 cold-heading-quality wire rod. Certified mill coils; every heat number traceable through the works.

02

Cold Heading

Head Formation

High-speed multi-station presses form bolt head geometry at room temperature. M3–M36, no material waste.

03

Thread Rolling

CNC Rolling

Threads rolled between hardened dies — pitch accuracy ±0.01 mm. Stronger than cut threads.

04

Heat Treatment

Quench & Temper

Continuous mesh-belt furnace. Controlled-atmosphere quench and temper to grades 4.8–12.9.

05

Surface Coating

In-House Lines

Zinc plating, hot-dip galvanising, Dacromet, black oxide. Salt spray tested to 1000 h in our lab.

06

Quality Inspection

Full Mechanical Test

Tensile, proof load, hardness, torque. Mill cert per batch; SGS / BV third-party inspection on request.

BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

What buyers
ask us first.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Stock DIN/ISO items: one carton. Made-to-order: typically 500 kg for common sizes, one tonne for larger diameters or non-standard parts needing tooling.

How long is the lead time?

Stock items ship in 7–10 days. Production runs 15–35 days depending on quantity and finish. HDG and Dacromet add roughly 5 days.

Can I get samples before ordering?

Yes — standard samples are free, you pay only the courier. Non-standard samples are made from your drawing; the cost comes off your first invoice if the order follows.

What are your payment terms?

30% T/T deposit, balance against copy B/L for first orders. Irrevocable L/C at sight accepted. Established customers move to open account.

Mill certificates and third-party inspection?

Every shipment carries a mill certificate traceable to the heat number. We welcome SGS, BV, TÜV or your own inspector — arranged before the container is sealed.

Can you make non-standard parts?

Yes — non-standard is normal work here, not an exception. Send a drawing (PDF, DWG, DXF, STEP) or a sample.
